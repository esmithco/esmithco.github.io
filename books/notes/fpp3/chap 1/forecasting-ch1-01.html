Forecasting — First Principles

Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd ed.) · OTexts, otexts.com/fpp3
Forecasting is predicting future — or cyclical — events, using science and data.
It's easier when —
  • 1 We have a good understanding of the factors that drive the phenomenon.
  • 2 We have lots of data.
  • 3 The past looks like the future. lottery vs. energy demand
  • 4 The forecast itself doesn't change the outcome it's predicting. weather vs. bank run ← the strong one
Three things worth remembering
  • # We can forecast both events and quantities.
  • * The method we choose depends on the four conditions above.
  • * Sometimes the point of forecasting isn't the number — it's understanding the process well enough to adapt, adjust, and improve it.
