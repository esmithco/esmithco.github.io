Three Ways to Model It

Every model answers one of two questions — what causes the behavior, or what will happen next. Some try to answer both.
Explanatory model — what causes it
Uses other information besides the main variable.
ED = f( Temperature, Economy, Population, Time of day, Error )
energy demand, explained by what drives it
Time series model — what will happen
Predicts from the variable's own past. ★ best, most of the time
EDt+1 = f( EDt, EDt-1, … , ED0, Error )
predicting from past behavior
Mixed model — both at once
Its own past, plus outside variables.
EDt+1 = f( EDt, Temperature, Population, … , Error )
can be hard to calculate
